TL;DR AI platforms like ChatGPT and Claude can suggest promo codes, but the codes are frequently expired, inaccurate or simply made up. AI language models don’t have real-time access to retailer systems, so any code they produce is based on training data that may be months or years out of date. The risks include wasted time, failed checkouts and, in rare cases, codes that look real but have never existed.
